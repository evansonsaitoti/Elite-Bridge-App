Privacy Policy

Elite Bridge and Elite Bridge Employer

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Elite Bridge Staffing ("Elite Bridge," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when authorized users access the Elite Bridge caregiver application, the Elite Bridge Employer application, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the app, account role, enabled features, and information entered by an authorized agency, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, role, profile details, authentication information, and account-security records.
  • Workforce and scheduling information: caregiver availability, shift postings, applications, assignments, call-outs, replacement offers, qualifications, and related staffing records.
  • Agency and operational information: agency profile information, service details, scheduling information, operational checklists, and records entered by authorized employer users.
  • Client-related scheduling information: limited information needed to coordinate an authorized service assignment, such as a client or care-recipient name, service type, service location, and scheduling details. Organizations using Elite Bridge are responsible for entering only information appropriate for the service and their legal obligations.
  • Location information: precise or approximate device location may be requested when an enabled feature requires location, such as a visit clock-in or clock-out workflow. Permission is controlled through the device operating system.
  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, application version, diagnostics, security events, and service logs needed to operate and protect the applications.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide staffing and scheduling workflows, synchronize caregiver and employer activity, support applications and assignments, manage call-outs and coverage requests, provide operational decision support, maintain security, troubleshoot services, provide customer support, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. AI-Assisted Decision Support

Elite Bridge Employer includes decision-support features such as Coverage Copilot, Compliance Copilot, and Ask Elite. These features may analyze operational information already available to an authorized agency account and present summaries, rankings, risk signals, or suggested next steps.

Elite Bridge does not use these features to automatically assign caregivers, approve or reject applicants, make employment decisions, change payroll, or make legal or compliance determinations without an authorized person taking the relevant action. Agencies are responsible for reviewing recommendations and making final decisions.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared as needed with authorized users of the organization using Elite Bridge and with service providers that support hosting, databases, authentication, application delivery, security, notifications, diagnostics, or other technical functions. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, our services, or our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to satisfy legitimate organizational recordkeeping needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect the service, and comply with applicable law. Retention periods may vary by record type and by the organization using the applications.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Mobile applications do not contain database passwords or other private database connection credentials. Authentication is required for protected shared-service operations. No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, however, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we control, subject to applicable law, legitimate organizational recordkeeping requirements, and the role of the organization that provided or manages the information. Device permissions, including location access, can be managed through device settings. Disabling a permission may limit a related feature.

8. Children’s Privacy

The applications are intended for authorized adult agency administrators, employers, and workers. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly provide child user accounts through these applications.

9. Third-Party Service Providers

Elite Bridge may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers for functions such as application hosting, database hosting, authentication, build and application delivery, security, notifications, diagnostics, or other technical operations. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security obligations.

10. Organization Responsibilities

Organizations using Elite Bridge are responsible for configuring the applications appropriately for their services, limiting access to authorized users, entering only information they are permitted to process, and complying with applicable employment, health-care, payer, contractual, privacy, and recordkeeping requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
