Privacy Policy

Elite Bridge mobile application

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Elite Bridge Staffing ("Elite Bridge," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use the Elite Bridge mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is configured and used, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, role, profile details, and login credentials.
  • Workforce information: staff records, availability, assigned shifts, applications, timesheets, clock-in and clock-out records, earnings estimates, and service requests.
  • Client and operational information: client names, service plans, scheduling details, visit notes, and agency operational records entered by authorized users.
  • Location information: approximate or precise location may be used when a user clocks in or out, where enabled and permitted by the user.
  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, app version, crash information, diagnostics, and security logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve the app, authenticate users, manage staffing and scheduling workflows, process clock-in and clock-out activity, maintain timesheets and operational records, provide support, protect accounts, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable law.

3. Demonstration Data

Some versions of the app may contain demonstration records used for testing, review, training, or onboarding. Users should not enter real sensitive personal, medical, or financial information into a demonstration environment.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with authorized agency administrators and staff, service providers that support app hosting, authentication, analytics, security, or communications, and government or legal authorities when required by law. We may also disclose information to protect users, the public, or our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to satisfy agency recordkeeping needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal requirements. Retention periods may vary by record type and the organization using the app.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also manage device permissions, including location access, through your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit related app features.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for authorized adult agency administrators and workers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through user accounts.

9. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party service providers for hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, or other technical functions. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices and are expected to use information only as necessary to provide services to us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means the revised policy applies.
