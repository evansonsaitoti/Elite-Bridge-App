## Description

Elite Bridge Caregiver is the care-professional side of the Elite Bridge staffing marketplace.

Caregivers can create a professional profile, manage matching preferences, receive eligible shift opportunities, claim instant assignments or apply for employer approval, report call-outs, and follow urgent replacement offers.

For confirmed assignments, the app provides a production-backed time clock with optional location capture at clock-in and clock-out, break tracking, visit notes, timesheet submission, employer approval and correction requests. Persistent in-app alerts and optional push notifications keep schedule and timesheet decisions visible.

Elite Bridge Caregiver connects to the separately listed Elite Bridge Employer app. Employers publish and manage shifts; caregivers receive and work them. Each app has distinct users, permissions and workflows while sharing the same secure marketplace service.
