Elite Bridge Privacy Policy

Staffing you can rely on.

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Elite Bridge Staffing ("Elite Bridge," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Elite Bridge mobile application and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how the app is configured and used, we may collect account details such as name, email address, role, profile information, availability, schedules, timesheets, clock-in and clock-out records, service notes, requests, and other information submitted through the app.

The app may request location access when a staff member clocks in or out. Location information is intended to support visit verification and is not collected continuously in the background.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to provide and improve the app, authenticate users, manage staffing and client-service workflows, support scheduling and timekeeping, communicate with users, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Demo and Test Data

Some versions of the app may include demonstration records for testing, onboarding, or App Store review. Users should not enter sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential client information into a demonstration environment.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized agency administrators, staff members, service providers that help operate the app, or government and legal authorities when required by law. Access is limited according to role and operational need.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Some app data may be stored locally on the user’s device, particularly in demonstration or local-first versions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet operational needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal or regulatory requirements. Locally stored app data may remain on a device until it is deleted, reset, or the app is removed.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also control device permissions, including location access, through your device settings.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Elite Bridge app is intended for authorized adult administrators and staff members. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the app.

9. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party hosting, analytics, authentication, notification, mapping, or distribution services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Elite Bridge Staffing at admin@elitebridge.com.
