Questions

Good to know before you choose.

Is Elite Bridge a medical provider?

Elite Bridge Staffing focuses on non-medical care and staffing coordination. Its software supports staffing workflows; it does not provide medical care or medical advice.

Can I use the same account on web and mobile?

Yes. The web portal and mobile app are designed to work with the same Elite Bridge account.

What happens in a software demo?

We’ll walk through the relevant workflows, learn about your team and answer questions about package fit. There’s no online purchase required.

Do you publish fixed software prices?

Package pricing is discussed based on your team and setup. Contact us and we’ll walk through the available options.

How do I open my existing workspace?
